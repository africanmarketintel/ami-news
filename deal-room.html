How the Deal Room works

ABTA Deal Room is a private, structured platform for qualified principals. It operates on a controlled disclosure model — identities are never revealed until ABTA has screened both parties and issued an Introduction Notice.

01
Registration & Document Acceptance

Create your Deal Room account (£99/year, separate from AMI subscription). Before access is granted, you review and accept the Platform Membership Agreement and Non-Disclosure Agreement using our document acceptance flow.

02
Browse Blind Teasers

All opportunities are anonymised. Sector, geography and financial attributes are visible. Counterparty names, contacts and identifying details are revealed only to Deal Room members who have completed the document acceptance process.

03
Confirm Interest

Submit a rationale (max 250 words) and your mandate details. ABTA reviews suitability, credibility and transaction fit before proceeding.

04
ABTA Screening & Introduction

If qualified, ABTA issues an Introduction Notice and discloses identities to both parties. An NDA between the parties is optional at this stage but recommended for deeper information sharing.

05
Direct Negotiation

Parties negotiate directly. All introductions remain subject to the ABTA Platform Membership Agreement. ABTA does not act as adviser or broker in the resulting transaction.

Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between the African Business Trade Association ("ABTA") and the registering member ("Member").

1. Confidential Information

For the purposes of this Agreement, "Confidential Information" means any information disclosed through the ABTA Deal Room platform, including but not limited to deal teasers, counterparty identities, financial data, business descriptions, transaction structures and any related materials.

2. Obligations

The Member agrees to: (a) hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not disclose Confidential Information to any third party without prior written consent from ABTA; (c) use Confidential Information solely for the purpose of evaluating potential transactions facilitated through the Deal Room; (d) notify ABTA immediately upon becoming aware of any unauthorised use or disclosure of Confidential Information.

3. Permitted Disclosures

The Member may disclose Confidential Information to its professional advisers, employees and agents who have a need to know for the purpose of evaluating a potential transaction, provided that such persons are bound by equivalent confidentiality obligations.

4. Exclusions

This Agreement does not apply to information that: (a) is or becomes publicly known through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was rightfully known to the Member before disclosure; (c) is independently developed by the Member without reference to the Confidential Information; or (d) is required to be disclosed by law or regulatory order.

5. Term

This Agreement remains in force for the duration of the Member's Deal Room membership and for a period of three (3) years thereafter.

6. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales. Any disputes shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of England and Wales.

By accepting this document, the Member confirms that they have read, understood and agree to be bound by the terms of this Non-Disclosure Agreement.

ABTA Deal Room Platform Membership Agreement

This Platform Membership Agreement ("Agreement") governs the Member's access to and use of the ABTA Deal Room platform ("Platform"), operated by the African Business Trade Association ("ABTA").

1. Membership

Membership of the Deal Room is separate from any AMI editorial subscription. The annual membership fee is £99 (ninety-nine pounds sterling), payable in advance. Membership grants access to the Platform for a period of twelve (12) months from the date of payment.

2. Platform Use

The Member may: (a) browse all active deal teasers, including revealed counterparty data; (b) submit interest confirmations for any opportunity; (c) submit capital opportunities for ABTA review; (d) access ABTA's structured introduction process subject to screening.

3. Screening and Introductions

ABTA reserves the right to decline any interest confirmation at its sole discretion. An introduction does not constitute a recommendation or endorsement by ABTA of either party or of the transaction. ABTA is not a financial adviser, broker or intermediary in any resulting transaction.

4. Submitted Opportunities

By submitting a capital opportunity, the Member grants ABTA a non-exclusive right to anonymise and publish the opportunity on the Platform. ABTA reserves the right to decline, edit or remove any submission at its sole discretion.

5. Fees and Refunds

The annual membership fee is non-refundable once access has been granted. ABTA reserves the right to vary the membership fee on renewal with 30 days' notice.

6. Termination

ABTA may terminate membership immediately and without refund if the Member breaches any term of this Agreement or the Non-Disclosure Agreement, or if ABTA reasonably determines that the Member is acting in bad faith.

7. Limitation of Liability

ABTA's liability to the Member in respect of any claim arising from use of the Platform shall not exceed the annual membership fee paid by the Member. ABTA shall not be liable for any indirect, consequential or economic loss.

8. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of England and Wales.

By accepting this document, the Member confirms that they have read, understood and agree to be bound by the terms of this Platform Membership Agreement.

Deal Room Fee Schedule

This Fee Schedule sets out the charges applicable to Deal Room membership as at 1 July 2026.
