    How the Deal Room works

    1. Browse anonymised opportunities

    See the sector, region, deal type and capital sought. Names and contact details are not shown.

    2. Express interest

    Members tell ABTA why an opportunity fits their mandate.

    3. ABTA screens and introduces

    ABTA reviews each request. If it is suitable, ABTA issues an Introduction Notice to both parties.

    Read the full process, timescales and fees

    Who the Deal Room is for

    The Deal Room is for organisations that invest in, lend to, acquire or partner with businesses and projects in African markets, including:

    • strategic investors and corporate buyers
    • private equity firms and family offices
    • development finance institutions
    • banks and alternative lenders
    • commercial trade partners

    Businesses, project sponsors and their advisers can also list opportunities for ABTA to review.

    Raising capital or selling a business?

    Members can submit an opportunity for review. If it meets ABTA’s criteria, ABTA contacts you to prepare an anonymised listing before it is published.
