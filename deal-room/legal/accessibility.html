Accessibility statement for the ABTA Deal Room

Draft · Prepared [date]

Draft for legal review – not in force. This version 2.0 draft has been prepared to close gaps found in the Deal Room audit. It is not legal advice. Wording in yellow brackets must be completed or confirmed by ABTA and its legal adviser before publication.

1. Using this service

We want as many people as possible to be able to use the Deal Room. You should be able to change colours, contrast and fonts using your browser or device settings, zoom in up to 400% without the text spilling off the screen, navigate using just a keyboard or speech recognition software and use a screen reader.

2. How accessible this service is

We designed the Deal Room to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2 at AA level, using patterns from the GOV.UK Design System. [Update after independent accessibility audit: list any known issues]

3. Feedback and contact

If you need information in a different format or have problems using the Deal Room, email ami@abta.africa. We aim to reply within 2 business days.
