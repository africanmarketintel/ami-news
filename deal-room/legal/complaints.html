Complaints

Draft · Last updated [date]

Draft for legal review – not in force. This version 2.0 draft has been prepared to close gaps found in the Deal Room audit. It is not legal advice. Wording in yellow brackets must be completed or confirmed by ABTA and its legal adviser before publication.

1. How to complain

Email ami@abta.africa with “Complaint” in the subject line. Tell us what happened, when it happened and what you would like us to do.

2. What happens next

  1. We acknowledge your complaint within [5] business days.
  2. A senior member of the ABTA team who was not involved reviews it.
  3. We send you a full response within [20] business days, or tell you why we need longer.

3. Data protection complaints

If your complaint is about how we use your personal data, we will acknowledge it within 30 days. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office.
