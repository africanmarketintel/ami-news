Cookies and browser storage

Draft · Last updated [date]

Draft for legal review – not in force. This version 2.0 draft has been prepared to close gaps found in the Deal Room audit. It is not legal advice. Wording in yellow brackets must be completed or confirmed by ABTA and its legal adviser before publication.

1. What we use

The Deal Room does not use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies, and does not load third-party fonts or scripts. It uses your browser’s storage only for things that are needed for the service to work.

2. Essential storage

NamePurposeHow long
ami_auth_token, ami_refresh_token, ami_token_expires, ami_user_emailKeeps you signed in and lets the site make secure requests for youUntil you sign out or the session expires
dr_interest, dr_submit, dr_joinRemembers your answers while you complete a formUntil you close the browser tab
dr_resultsRemembers your last search so “Back to results” worksUntil you close the browser tab

[Legal adviser to confirm these fall within the strictly necessary exemption]

3. Payments

When you pay, you are taken to Stripe’s checkout page, which sets its own cookies for security and fraud prevention. See Stripe’s cookie policy.
