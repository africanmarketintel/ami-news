This summary helps you understand the document. The full text below is the binding version.

1. Annual membership fee

£99 per year, [including VAT / plus VAT – confirm VAT treatment]. Payable in advance by debit or credit card through our payment provider, Stripe. Membership starts when payment is confirmed and lasts 12 months.

2. Renewal

Membership renews automatically at the then-current fee. We will email you at least 30 days before the renewal date with the date and price. You can turn off renewal at any time before the renewal date in Your Deal Room, or by emailing ami@abta.africa.

3. Introduction and success fees

[Option A – There is no fee for Interest Confirmations, introductions or any resulting Transaction.]

[Option B – A success fee of [x]% of [Transaction value, defined as …] is payable by [the investing Member / the submitting Member] within [30] days of completion of a Transaction with an Introduced Party, subject to a minimum of [amount] and maximum of [amount].]

ABTA must choose one option. The on-page text of the Deal Room and the Platform Membership Agreement will be updated to match.

4. Submitting an opportunity

There is no extra charge to submit an opportunity for review or to publish an approved listing.

5. Refunds

[Refund position – for example, a full refund if you cancel within 14 days of first joining and have not submitted an Interest Confirmation or opportunity; otherwise no refund for the remainder of the membership year]. This does not affect any rights you have by law.
