Deal Room privacy notice

Draft · Last updated [date]

Draft for legal review – not in force. This version 2.0 draft has been prepared to close gaps found in the Deal Room audit. It is not legal advice. Wording in yellow brackets must be completed or confirmed by ABTA and its legal adviser before publication.

In plain terms

  • We collect details about you and your organisation to run the Deal Room, verify members and make introductions.
  • We share personal data only with service providers who process it for us and with parties we introduce you to. We also share it where the law requires.
  • Some checks use Google’s Gemini AI service. They are advisory and a person at ABTA makes every decision.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your data.

This summary helps you understand the document. The full text below is the binding version.

1. Who we are

The data controller is [legal name of operating entity], [company number], whose registered office is at [registered office address] (“ABTA”, “we”, “us”). Contact: ami@abta.africa. ICO registration number: [number].

2. What we collect

  • Account details: name, work email address, job title, password (stored in hashed form by our authentication provider).
  • Organisation details: registered name, country, registration number, organisation type.
  • Payment records: amount, date and payment status. Card details are handled by Stripe and are not seen or stored by ABTA.
  • Deal Room activity: opportunities you submit and documents you upload, Interest Confirmations, questions, saved opportunities and alert preferences.
  • Agreement records: the version of each agreement you accept, the time, email address and IP address.
  • Verification records: the checks we carry out on your organisation, including sanctions screening results.
  • Technical data: IP address and browser type, used for security, spam prevention and audit logs.

3. Why we use it and our lawful basis

Verifying organisations and sanctions screening[Legal obligation / legitimate interests – confirm]

Weekly opportunity alerts you choose to receive[Consent / legitimate interests – confirm]

4. Use of AI

When you use the “check for identifying details” tool on an opportunity summary, and when we screen Interest Confirmations and submissions for spam, the text is sent to Google’s Gemini API. They do not make decisions about you: an ABTA staff member reviews every submission and request.
