African Business Trade Association · Deal Room

Fee Schedule

Version 1.0 · Effective 1 July 2026

This Fee Schedule sets out the charges applicable to Deal Room membership as at 1 July 2026.

Annual Membership Fee

£99
per year · inclusive of VAT where applicable

Payable in advance by credit or debit card via Stripe. Membership is activated on receipt of cleared payment and grants access for twelve (12) months from the date of payment.

Introductions

There is no separate fee for interest confirmations or introductions facilitated through the Platform. ABTA does not charge a success fee or transaction fee on any deal resulting from a Deal Room introduction.

Deal Submission

Submitting a capital opportunity for ABTA review is included within the annual membership fee. There is no additional charge for submission, review or publication.

Renewal

Membership renews annually. ABTA will notify members by email at least 30 days before renewal. The renewal fee will be the then-current annual membership fee.

Refund Policy

The annual membership fee is non-refundable once access to the Platform has been granted, except where required by applicable consumer protection law.

Electronic Acceptance

By scrolling through this document and selecting the acceptance checkbox within the ABTA Deal Room registration flow, the Member confirms that they have read and understood the applicable fee schedule. This acceptance constitutes a legally valid electronic signature under the Electronic Communications Act 2000.
