Who runs it

ABTA operates the platform and is accountable for what leaves its sending domain.

Checked automatically

The platform checks each campaign before it goes, holds you to a monthly allowance and watches complaint and bounce rates. It suspends an account automatically when those rates pass the level mailbox providers accept.

Held only when serious

A campaign is held for a person to look at only when a check finds something serious: a request for money, or an image with no description. ABTA looks at held campaigns within one working day.

Your list stays yours

ABTA does not write members' campaigns and does not own their lists. For a member's subscriber data ABTA acts as a processor and the member is the controller. The data processing terms set that out.
