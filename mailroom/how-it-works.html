How AMI Mailroom works

For senders

  1. Sign up

    Tell us who you are, the postal address that will appear in every email, and how your list was collected. Verify your email address and you can send: nobody approves you first.

  2. Record your consent evidence

    For each list: how the addresses were collected, what people agreed to, and when. A list without that cannot be mailed.

  3. Write the campaign

    Blocks, not a page builder, so what arrives is light, accessible and works with images off.

  4. Test, then send

    Send a test to your own team, check it on a phone and with images off, then send, or pick a date and a time and leave it to go on its own. There is no daily cap: a list of three thousand can go out on your first day, within your monthly allowance.

  5. Read the results

    Delivered, bounced, clicked, unsubscribed and complaints. Open rates are not shown, because mail privacy features make them unreliable.

For readers

Every campaign carries the sender's name and postal address, a link to choose what you receive, and an unsubscribe link that works in one click.

Unsubscribing stops that sender. There is also one link that stops every sender using AMI Mailroom.

If an email asks you for money or you never signed up, reply to it or write to ami@abta.africa. ABTA can suspend the sender.

What ABTA does

ABTA operates the platform and is accountable for what leaves its sending domain. You can also send from a domain of your own, which keeps your delivery record separate from every other sender: see where your email comes from. Nobody approves your campaigns. Instead the platform checks each one automatically before it goes, holds you to a monthly allowance, watches complaint and bounce rates, and suspends an account automatically when those rates pass the level mailbox providers accept.

A campaign is held for a person to look at only when a check finds something serious: a request for money, an image with no description, or a missing postal address. ABTA looks at held campaigns within one working day.

ABTA does not write members' campaigns and does not own their lists. For a member's subscriber data ABTA acts as a processor, and the member is the controller. The data processing terms set that out.
