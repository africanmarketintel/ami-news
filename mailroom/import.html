Add your subscribers

Upload a CSV, TXT or Excel file, or paste the addresses in. Name, organisation and country are optional.

What happens to your file

Every address is checked against the blocked addresses. Addresses that do not exist, people who reported spam and anyone who asked to stop all AMI Mailroom email are skipped. Someone who unsubscribed from your own list stays unsubscribed.

CSV, TXT or Excel (.xlsx). A first row headed email brings in name, organisation and country columns too.
One per line, or separated by commas. A name in front also works, as in Ada Obi <ada@example.com>. You can paste straight from a spreadsheet: if the first row is headed email, the name, organisation and country columns are read too. You can use this as well as a file.

How were these addresses collected? The same question your list answers, asked again because a file can come from somewhere the rest of the list did not.
