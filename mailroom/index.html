Send email your subscribers asked for

AMI Mailroom lets ABTA members write and send campaigns to their own lists, through infrastructure ABTA maintains and monitors.

Create a sender account How it works

  • Send as soon as you sign up, with no daily cap
  • Consent recorded for every list
  • One-click unsubscribe in every email
  • Complaints watched across the platform

What you get

Lists you control

Import your own subscribers, record how they were collected, and keep the evidence in one place.

Campaigns that arrive

Authenticated sending, a suppression list shared across the platform, and results that tell you what happened.

Readers in charge

A preference centre on every email, so people choose topics and frequency instead of leaving.

What it is not

It is not a bulk email tool. Lists must be your own, collected with consent you can evidence. Bought, scraped or swapped lists are refused, and an account that sends to one is suspended.
