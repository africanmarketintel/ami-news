Your other tools

Results that mean something

Clicks, delivery and spam reports for every campaign. Open rates are not shown because mail privacy features make them unreliable.

Readers in charge

A page linked from every email where people choose what they get and how often, instead of leaving.

Email that proves who it is from

People who asked to stop, and addresses that do not exist, are blocked for you.

Only for people who asked.

It is not a bulk email tool. Lists must be your own, with people who agreed to hear from you. Bought, scraped or swapped lists are refused, and an account that sends to one is suspended.
