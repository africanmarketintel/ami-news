AMI Mailroom Acceptable use policy

Acceptable use policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 28 October 2026

In plain terms

Send to people who asked to hear from you, about what they expected, and make leaving easy. Do not send anything that harms, deceives or defrauds readers.

1. Who this applies to

This policy applies to every AMI Mailroom account and to everything sent from it. It forms part of the terms for senders.

2. Consent and lists

Send only to addresses that were given to you directly, by people who agreed to hear from you about the kind of email you are sending.

Do not use purchased, rented, scraped, appended or swapped lists. Do not use addresses collected from websites, directories, business cards or events where people did not opt in to your emails.

Do not send to an address that has unsubscribed, bounced permanently or reported your email as spam. Do not send to people who told you they did not sign up.

Keep a record of how, when and on what wording each list was collected, and show it to ABTA when asked.

3. Honest sending

Use your real organisation name. Do not imitate another person or organisation, or use a misleading sender name, subject line or reply address.

Keep the unsubscribe link in every email. Do not hide, disguise or break it, and do not make leaving harder than joining.

Follow the law that applies to your readers, including any rule that requires a postal address in marketing email, and any rule about emailing businesses or individuals.

4. Content that is never allowed

Requests for money, bank details, passwords or identity documents.

Fraud, scams, phishing, advance-fee schemes or anything that misleads readers about who you are or what you offer.

Financial promotions that need authorisation you do not hold, and anything presenting an investment return as guaranteed.

Malware, or links to pages that carry it or that imitate another organisation.

Content that is unlawful, discriminatory, defamatory, threatening or harassing, or that infringes someone else’s rights.

Adult content, gambling promotions and anything unlawful in the country of the person receiving it.

Special category personal data about your subscribers, for example about their health, religion or political views, uploaded to AMI Mailroom.

5. Technical rules

Do not add tracking beyond what AMI Mailroom provides, hidden images from third parties, scripts or attachments.

Every image needs a description, and the email must make sense with images off.

Do not try to avoid the automatic checks, the sending limits or a suspension, for example by opening another account.

6. What ABTA checks

Every campaign is checked automatically when it is sent. Some campaigns are held for a person at ABTA to review. Some are flagged and sent, for example a subject line in capitals or full of exclamation marks.

ABTA may ask you to prove that your readers agreed to receive your email. A sender who cannot do so may have sending stopped.

7. What happens if you breach this policy

ABTA decides what to do in proportion to the harm. A first minor breach usually means your campaigns are held for review for a period and you are told what to change. A serious breach, particularly asking readers for money or mailing a list you cannot show people joined, means immediate suspension. Repeated breaches, or the use of the service for fraud, mean the account is closed.

ABTA may remove content, tell affected readers what happened, and report unlawful activity to the authorities.

You can ask for any decision to be reviewed under the complaints process.

8. Reporting abuse

If you received an email sent through AMI Mailroom that breaks this policy, use the unsubscribe link and tell us at ami@abta.africa with the sender name and the date. We will look into it and tell you what we have done where the law allows.

9. Changes to this policy

ABTA may update this policy. Senders are told of changes by email at least 30 days before they apply, unless the change is needed sooner to comply with the law or to stop abuse.
