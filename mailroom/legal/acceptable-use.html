AMI Mailroom Acceptable use policy

Acceptable use policy

Version 0.1 draft · 14 September 2026

In plain terms

Send to people who asked to hear from you, about what they expected, and make leaving easy.

1. Consent

Only addresses given to you directly, for the kind of email you are sending. No purchased, rented, scraped, appended or swapped lists, and no addresses harvested from websites or events people did not opt in at.

2. Content that is never allowed

Requests for money, bank details or identity documents.

Financial promotions that would need authorisation the sender does not hold, and anything presenting a return as guaranteed.

Misleading subject lines, false sender names, or hidden unsubscribe links.

Content that is unlawful, discriminatory, defamatory or harassing.

Adult content, gambling promotions, and anything unlawful in the country of the recipient.

3. Technical rules

No tracking beyond what the platform provides, no hidden images from third parties, no scripts, no attachments.

Every image needs a description, and the email must make sense with images off.

4. What happens if you breach this

A first breach usually means your campaigns are held for review for a period. A serious breach, particularly asking readers for money or mailing a list you cannot evidence, means immediate suspension.

ABTA may tell affected subscribers what happened.
