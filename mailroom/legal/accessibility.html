AMI Mailroom Accessibility statement

Accessibility statement

Version 0.1 draft · 14 September 2026

DRAFT. This document has not been reviewed by a lawyer and is not in force. It is published for review only.

In plain terms

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA on the pages and in the emails we send.

1. Scope

This statement covers the AMI Mailroom pages at ami.abta.africa/mailroom, and the emails sent through it.

2. The pages

Built to the GOV.UK Design System patterns, with visible focus, error summaries, labelled fields and keyboard operation throughout.

Not yet audited independently. We will publish the result when it has been.

3. The emails

Campaigns are built from blocks that produce a single column of real text, so they work with images off, at large text sizes, and with a screen reader.

Every image requires a description before a campaign can be sent.

4. Feedback

Email ami@abta.africa if you find a barrier. Tell us the page or the campaign and what happened, and we will reply within five working days.
