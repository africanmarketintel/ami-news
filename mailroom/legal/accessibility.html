AMI Mailroom Accessibility statement

Accessibility statement

Version 1.0 · Effective 28 October 2026

In plain terms

We aim to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA on the pages and in the emails we send. This statement says where we have got to, what we know does not yet work, and how to tell us about a barrier.

1. Scope

This statement covers the AMI Mailroom pages at ami.abta.africa/mailroom and the emails sent through it. It is published by African Business Trade Association Limited.

2. How accessible the pages are

We aim to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. This is the standard recommended by the World Wide Web Consortium.

The pages follow the GOV.UK Design System patterns, with visible keyboard focus, error summaries, labelled form fields and operation by keyboard throughout.

The pages have not yet been audited by an independent body, so we cannot say that they fully meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. We will publish the result of an independent audit when we have one.

3. The emails

Campaigns are built from blocks that produce a single column of real text, so they work with images off, at large text sizes and with a screen reader.

Every image needs a description before a campaign can be sent. We cannot check that the description is useful, so we ask senders to write one that does the job of the image.

4. Known limits

The campaign editor uses drag and drop. If you cannot use it, email us and we will help you build a campaign another way.

Some email apps do not load custom fonts, show images or support dark mode in the same way, and we cannot control that.

5. Feedback and requests

Email ami@abta.africa if you find a barrier, or if you need something in another format. Tell us the page or campaign and what happened. We will reply within five working days.

6. If you are not satisfied

If you are not happy with our reply, ask for it to be reviewed under the complaints process.

7. This statement

This statement was prepared on 28 October 2026. We review it at least once a year and whenever the pages change in a way that affects it.
