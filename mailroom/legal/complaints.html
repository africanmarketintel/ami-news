Complaints

Version 0.1 draft · 14 September 2026

DRAFT. This document has not been reviewed by a lawyer and is not in force. It is published for review only.

In plain terms

Tell us about an email you did not ask for and we will act on it, including suspending the sender.

1. If you received an email you did not ask for

Use the unsubscribe link first: it works in one click and stops that sender at once.

Then tell us at ami@abta.africa, with the sender name and the date. ABTA can require the sender to prove consent, and can suspend the account while it looks.

2. If you are a sender

Complaints about a decision, a suspension or a limit go to ami@abta.africa. We acknowledge within two working days and answer within ten.

3. If you are not satisfied

Ask for the complaint to be reviewed by someone who has not dealt with it.

For data protection complaints you can also contact the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).
