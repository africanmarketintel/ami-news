Complaints

Version 1.0 · Effective 28 October 2026

In plain terms

Tell us about an email you did not ask for and we will act on it, including suspending the sender. If you are a sender, you can ask us to review any decision.

1. If you received an email you did not ask for

Use the unsubscribe link first. It works in one click and stops that sender at once.

Then tell us at ami@abta.africa, with the sender name and the date. ABTA can require the sender to prove that you agreed to receive it, and can suspend the account while it looks.

We acknowledge your report within two working days and tell you what we have done where the law allows.

2. If you are a sender

Complaints about a decision, a held campaign, a suspension, a limit or a charge go to ami@abta.africa. Say what happened, what you want us to do and why you think the decision is wrong.

We acknowledge within two working days and answer in full within ten working days. If we need longer we will say why and when you will hear from us.

Held campaigns are looked at within one working day. This process is for reviewing a decision, so you do not need to use it to ask what to change.

3. If you are not satisfied

Ask for the complaint to be reviewed by someone who was not involved. We will answer within ten working days and tell you the final outcome in writing.

For complaints about personal data you can also contact the supervisory authority where you live or work. In the United Kingdom this is the Information Commissioner’s Office. See the privacy policy for others.

If the matter is a dispute with ABTA about the service, the terms for senders set out how disputes are dealt with.

4. What we do with complaints

We record every complaint and what we did about it. We use them to find sending that harms readers, and to improve the service. We use the details you give only to deal with your complaint.
