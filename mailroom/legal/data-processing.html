AMI Mailroom Data processing terms

Data processing terms

Version 0.1 draft · 14 September 2026

In plain terms

Your subscribers are your data. ABTA processes it only to send what you ask it to send, keeps it secure, and hands it back or deletes it when you leave.

1. Roles

For subscriber data uploaded by a sender, the sender is the controller and ABTA is the processor.

For sender account data, and for platform-wide suppression records, ABTA is the controller in its own right.

2. Subject matter and duration

Processing lasts for as long as the sender has an account, and for the retention periods set out below.

The processing is: storing subscriber records, segmenting them, sending campaigns, recording delivery events, and handling unsubscribes.

3. Types of data and people

Contact details and preferences of the sender’s subscribers: name, email address, organisation, country, chosen topics and frequency, and the record of their consent.

No special category data may be uploaded to AMI Mailroom.

4. ABTA’s obligations

Process only on the sender’s documented instructions, which these terms and use of the platform constitute.

Keep the data secure, with access limited to staff who need it.

Assist the sender with subscriber requests, and with any consultation or breach notification the sender must make.

Tell the sender without undue delay on becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting their data.

5. Sub-processors

ABTA uses providers for cloud hosting and database services, and for email delivery. A current list is available on request, and ABTA will tell senders before adding or replacing one.

6. International transfers

Data may be processed outside the United Kingdom. Where it is, ABTA relies on appropriate safeguards.

7. Suppression records

When someone unsubscribes or complains, ABTA keeps their address in a suppression record so that no sender mails them again. That record is kept indefinitely, as the only way to honour the request, and holds nothing beyond the address, the reason and the date.

8. Return and deletion

On request, or when an account closes, ABTA returns the sender’s subscriber data in a portable format and deletes it, except the suppression records above and what it must keep by law.

9. Audit

ABTA will provide the information needed to show compliance with these terms, and allow an audit on reasonable notice.
