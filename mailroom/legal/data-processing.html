AMI Mailroom Data processing terms

Data processing terms

Version 1.0 · Effective 28 October 2026

In plain terms

Your subscribers are your data. ABTA processes it only to send what you ask it to send, keeps it secure, tells you quickly if something goes wrong, and hands it back or deletes it when you leave.

1. Scope and roles

These terms are part of the terms for senders and apply whenever African Business Trade Association Limited (“ABTA”) processes personal data on behalf of a sender (“you”). They are written to meet Article 28 of the UK GDPR and the equivalent rules in the EU GDPR.

For subscriber data that you upload or collect through AMI Mailroom, you are the controller and ABTA is your processor.

For sender account data, for platform-wide suppression records and for what it needs to detect abuse and protect the service, ABTA is a controller in its own right. This is described in the privacy policy.

2. What the processing is

Subject matter and purpose: storing subscriber records, organising them into lists and segments, sending campaigns you create, recording delivery events and handling unsubscribes.

Duration: for as long as you have an account, and for the retention periods in section 8.

People: your subscribers and the contacts you upload.

Types of data: name, email address, organisation, country, chosen topics and frequency, the record of how and when the person joined, and delivery events for each email (delivered, bounced, reported as spam, link clicked, unsubscribed). No special category data may be uploaded to AMI Mailroom.

3. Your instructions

ABTA will process personal data only on your documented instructions. These terms, your use of the platform and the settings you choose are your instructions. ABTA will tell you if it believes an instruction breaches data protection law.

You must make sure you have a lawful basis for the processing, that your subscribers have been given the information the law requires, and that your instructions comply with the law.

4. ABTA’s obligations

ABTA will make sure that everyone who can access the data is bound by a duty of confidence and has access only where they need it.

ABTA will keep the data secure, using measures appropriate to the risk. These include access limited to staff who need it, separation of each sender’s data from other senders’, encryption of data in transit.

ABTA will help you respond to requests from people exercising their rights, and with any data protection impact assessment or consultation with a regulator that concerns your use of the platform, taking into account the information ABTA holds.

ABTA will tell you without undue delay, and in any event within 48 hours, after becoming aware of a personal data breach affecting your data. The notice will describe what happened, the data and people affected where known and what ABTA is doing about it.

5. Sub-processors

You give ABTA general authority to use the sub-processors below. ABTA has a contract with each that requires the same level of protection as these terms, and remains responsible for their performance.

6. International transfers

ABTA is based in the United Kingdom. Some sub-processors store or process data outside the United Kingdom, including in the European Economic Area and the United States.

ABTA will transfer personal data only where the transfer is covered by UK adequacy regulations or by an appropriate safeguard recognised by UK law, such as the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses. Where your data is subject to the EU GDPR, the EU standard contractual clauses apply in the same way.

7. Suppression records

When someone unsubscribes, a sender’s emails to an address bounce permanently, or someone reports a campaign as spam, ABTA keeps the address in a suppression record so that it is not mailed again. The record holds the address, the reason and the date, and nothing else.

ABTA keeps suppression records for as long as is needed to honour the person’s request. This is the only way to do so, because deleting the address would allow it to be mailed again. ABTA is the controller of these records.

8. Return and deletion

You can export your subscriber data at any time from your account.

Send and delivery records are deleted 24 months after they were created. When an account is closed, ABTA deletes the subscriber data, lists, send and delivery records, drafts, images and saved blocks for that account straight away. Sent campaigns stay in the archive unless you ask for them to be removed.

Data may stay in the backups of a sub-processor until they expire in the ordinary way. It is not used for any other purpose in that time.

ABTA may keep personal data where the law requires it, and the suppression records described above.

9. Audit and information

ABTA will give you the information you reasonably need to show that these terms are being followed, and will allow audits, including inspections, by you or an auditor you appoint, on reasonable notice and during working hours, no more than once a year unless a breach has occurred or a regulator requires it. You bear the cost.

10. Regulators and requests

If ABTA receives a request or complaint about your subscribers’ data directly, it will tell you promptly and will not respond except to say that it has passed it on, unless the law requires otherwise or you have asked it to.

11. Liability, law and changes

Liability under these terms is subject to the limits in the terms for senders. They are governed by the law of England and Wales.

ABTA may update these terms where the law or its practice changes, by giving senders at least 30 days’ notice by email. The version and date at the top show which one applies.
