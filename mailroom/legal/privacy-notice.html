AMI Mailroom Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Version 1.0 · Effective 28 October 2026

In plain terms

If you receive a campaign, the sender decides what you get and ABTA sends it for them. If you are a sender, ABTA holds your account details. You can ask either of us for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted.

1. Who we are

AMI Mailroom is operated by African Business Trade Association Limited (company number 16401954), whose registered office is at Fifth Floor, 167–169 Great Portland Street, London, England, W1W 5PF (“ABTA”, “we”, “us”). It is a service of African Market Intelligence.

This policy covers AMI Mailroom. The AMI privacy policy covers the AMI website, the newsletter and your AMI account across all our services, and applies alongside this one. Where the two differ for AMI Mailroom, this policy applies.

Privacy enquiries: ami@abta.africa.

2. If you receive a campaign

The sender named in the email decided to send it to you and holds your details. The sender is the controller of that data, and ABTA sends the email for them as their processor. Ask the sender about why they hold your address and what they do with it.

ABTA records what happened to each email it sends: whether it was delivered, bounced, was reported as spam, whether a link was clicked and whether you unsubscribed. ABTA holds this on the sender’s behalf and uses it to run the service and to detect abuse.

To stop the emails, use the unsubscribe link in the email. It works in one click. You can also ask ABTA at ami@abta.africa to stop all AMI Mailroom email to your address.

Ask the sender for a copy of your data or to have it deleted. If you cannot reach them, tell us and we will pass your request on, and will act ourselves if the sender does not.

If you did not ask for an email, tell us. See the complaints process.

3. If you are a sender

ABTA is the controller of the account information it holds about you. We collect it from you, and from your use of the service.

We hold: the name and email address of the people who use your account; your organisation name, website and postal address; your sending domains and reply addresses; your plan and billing records; the records of how your lists were collected; and your sending history, checks, holds, suspensions and support exchanges.

We use it to run the account and send your campaigns, to take payment, to check campaigns and protect other senders and readers, to keep the platform secure and to meet our legal duties.

4. Suppression records

When anyone unsubscribes, a sender’s emails to an address bounce permanently, or someone reports a campaign as spam, we keep the address in a suppression record so that it is not mailed again. The record holds the address, the reason and the date. We keep it for as long as is needed to honour the request, because deleting it would allow the address to be mailed again.

6. Who we share personal data with

We do not sell personal data, and no list is shared between senders.

We use service providers that process personal data on our instructions: Supabase (database, sign-in and server functions), Amazon Web Services (Amazon SES, which delivers email from Stockholm, Sweden) and GitHub (which serves the web pages). The full list, with what each does, is in the data processing terms.

Where a sender uses photo or GIF search, the words they type are sent to the image provider they choose. The pricing page may ask an exchange-rate service for rates, to show prices in your currency, and the service receives the request your browser makes, including your IP address. Neither service receives subscriber data.

We share personal data where the law requires it, or to establish or defend legal claims.

7. Transfers outside the UK

Some of our providers store or process personal data outside the United Kingdom, including in the European Economic Area and the United States. Where they do, we rely on UK adequacy regulations or on safeguards recognised by UK law, such as the International Data Transfer Agreement or the UK Addendum to the EU standard contractual clauses. Ask us at ami@abta.africa for details.

9. Security

We protect personal data with measures that are appropriate to the risk. They include access limited to the people who need it, separation of each sender’s data from other senders’, encryption of data in transit. If a breach puts people at risk we will tell the affected senders within 48 hours, and the regulator and the people affected where the law requires.

10. Cookies and browser storage

The AMI Mailroom pages use your browser’s storage to keep a sender signed in and to remember choices. They also use Google Analytics to see how the pages are used, as described in section 5 of the AMI privacy policy, where you can also find how to turn it off. Emails sent through AMI Mailroom carry no tracking pixel from ABTA. Clicks on links are recorded.

11. Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of your personal data, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how it is used, to receive it in a portable form and, where we rely on consent, to withdraw it. You also have an absolute right to object to direct marketing.

Email ami@abta.africa. We will respond within one month. We may ask you to confirm who you are. If your data is held by a sender, we will pass your request on to them.

We do not make decisions about people based only on automated processing that have a legal or similarly significant effect on them. Campaigns are checked automatically and may be held for a person to review. A sender can ask for any decision to be reviewed.

12. Complaints

Please contact us first so that we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the supervisory authority where you live or work. In the United Kingdom this is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk). Other authorities include the Nigeria Data Protection Commission, the Information Regulator of South Africa and the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner of Kenya.

13. Changes to this policy

We update this policy when what we do changes. The version and date at the top show when. Where a change materially affects you, we will tell you.
