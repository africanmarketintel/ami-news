AMI Mailroom Privacy policy

Privacy policy

Version 0.1 draft · 14 September 2026

DRAFT. This document has not been reviewed by a lawyer and is not in force. It is published for review only.

In plain terms

For campaign subscribers, the sender decides what you get and ABTA sends it. For senders, ABTA holds the account details. You can ask either of us for a copy of your data or for it to be deleted.

1. Who we are

AMI Mailroom is operated by African Business Trade Association Limited (company number 16401954), whose registered office is at Fifth Floor, 167–169 Great Portland Street, London, England, W1W 5PF (“ABTA”, “we”, “us”). Privacy enquiries: ami@abta.africa.

2. If you receive a campaign

The sender named in the email decided to send it and holds your details. ABTA sends it for them, as their processor.

ABTA records delivery events: whether the message was delivered, bounced, was reported as spam, and whether a link was clicked.

To stop the emails, use the unsubscribe link, or the preference page to change what you receive. There is also a link that stops every sender using AMI Mailroom.

Ask the sender for a copy of your data or for deletion. If you cannot reach them, tell ABTA and we will pass it on and act ourselves if they do not.

3. If you are a sender

ABTA holds your organisation name, postal address, contact details, sending records and account history, to run the service, approve senders and protect the sending domain.

4. Suppression records

When anyone unsubscribes or reports a campaign as spam, ABTA keeps that address in a suppression record so no sender mails them again. This is the only way to honour the request, so it is kept indefinitely.

5. Legal bases

Contract, for running a sender’s account.

Legitimate interests, for protecting deliverability, preventing abuse and keeping suppression records.

Consent, for campaigns themselves, which the sender is responsible for obtaining.

6. Who we share with

Categories of processor: cloud hosting and database providers, and email delivery providers. Nothing is sold, and no list is shared between senders.

7. How long we keep it

Subscriber records: while the sender keeps them, then deleted when the account closes.

Send and delivery event records: 24 months.

Campaign content: kept for the archive, unless the sender asks for it to be removed.

Suppression records: indefinitely, as above.

8. Your rights

Access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection and portability. Email ami@abta.africa and we will respond within one month.

9. Complaints

Please contact us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).
