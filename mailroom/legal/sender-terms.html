Terms for senders

Version 1.0 · Effective 28 October 2026

In plain terms

You own your list and what you send. ABTA runs the platform and can stop your sending if your mail harms readers or delivery. Read the rules before you send, because they are written to protect your readers and your own sending record.

1. Who we are and what these terms cover

AMI Mailroom is operated by African Business Trade Association Limited (company number 16401954), whose registered office is at Fifth Floor, 167–169 Great Portland Street, London, England, W1W 5PF (“ABTA”, “we”, “us”). It is part of African Market Intelligence, an ABTA service.

These terms are an agreement between ABTA and the person or organisation that creates an AMI Mailroom account (“you”). They apply to the AMI Mailroom pages, the sending service and anything you create with them.

The acceptable use policy, the data processing terms and the plans and sending rules on the pricing page form part of these terms. If they conflict, the data processing terms prevail on personal data and the acceptable use policy prevails on what you may send.

2. Accepting these terms

You accept these terms when you create an account or send for the first time, whichever is earlier.

AMI Mailroom is for business and professional use. If you accept for an organisation, you confirm that you have authority to bind it.

You must be at least 18 years old.

3. Your account

You must give accurate details and keep them up to date. You are responsible for what happens under your account and for keeping your sign-in secure. Tell us at once if you think someone else has had access.

You can send once you have created an account and confirmed your email address. ABTA does not approve senders in advance, but every campaign is checked as set out in section 8.

4. Plans, limits and fees

The plans, prices and limits are those shown on the pricing page when you choose a plan. Each account has a limit on emails sent and on subscribers held. Sending stops when a limit is reached. You are not charged for sending beyond a limit.

Fees, the billing period and the cancellation terms for a paid plan are shown when you choose it and form part of these terms. Prices include or exclude tax as stated at that point.

The 14-day trial has its own limits, shown on the pricing page. When it ends, sending stops and your account, lists and campaigns stay as they are until you choose a plan or close the account.

We will give you at least 30 days’ notice by email before changing the price of a plan you have already bought, and the change applies from your next billing period.

5. Your list and your campaigns

You are the sender of every campaign you send. You decide who receives it and what it says.

You confirm that every address on your list was given to you directly, by a person who agreed to hear from you about the kind of email you send, and that you can show when and how. None may be bought, rented, scraped, appended or swapped. For each list you must record how the addresses were collected, what people agreed to and when.

You must comply with the laws that apply to your email, including the laws of the countries where your readers are. These include, for example, the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations and UK GDPR, the EU ePrivacy rules and GDPR, the US CAN-SPAM Act, the Canadian Anti-Spam Legislation and the data protection laws of the African countries where you have readers.

Your campaigns must carry your organisation’s name and an unsubscribe link, which ABTA adds automatically. You must not remove, hide or break the link. Some countries also require a postal address in marketing email. You can add one in your account, and you are responsible for including it wherever the law requires it.

You must act on unsubscribes at once and must not add anyone back who has left, unless they ask you to in a way you can evidence.

The acceptable use policy sets out what you must not send.

6. Sign-up forms

A sign-up form you create with AMI Mailroom adds a person to your list as soon as they submit it. ABTA does not send a confirmation email first.

You are responsible for the wording of your form, for making clear what people are signing up to and for making sure the address belongs to the person who entered it. If someone tells you that they did not sign up, you must remove them straight away.

ABTA may require you to turn on a confirmation step, or to stop using a form, if it is being used to add people who did not ask to be added.

7. Where your email comes from

Every account sends from an address on a domain. By default this is an address on the ABTA sending domain that ABTA builds for your account. It is used by your account alone, so your sending record belongs to you.

You may instead send from a domain you own, on a paid plan. You must control the domain you add. You authorise ABTA and its email providers to send on your behalf from it for as long as it is on your account.

You are responsible for publishing and keeping the DNS records for your own domain. If they are removed, sending from that domain stops.

Replies go to the reply-to address you have confirmed. You must not use an address you do not control.

8. Checks, suspension and ending your use

Every campaign is checked automatically when it is sent. A campaign may be held until a person at ABTA has looked at it, for example where it appears to ask readers for money or bank details, or contains an image with no description. ABTA looks at held campaigns within one working day and tells you what to change.

ABTA stops sending automatically when the rate of spam reports or undeliverable addresses on a campaign passes the thresholds published on the pricing page. ABTA may also suspend an account or a domain, or remove a campaign, if you breach these terms or the acceptable use policy, or if that is needed to protect readers, other senders or the service.

Where we can, we will tell you the reason and give you the chance to put it right. We may suspend first and explain afterwards where a delay would harm readers or the delivery of other senders’ email.

Suspension is not permanent. Tell us what changed and ABTA can reinstate the account. You can ask for any suspension or removal to be reviewed under the complaints process.

9. Your content and our service

You keep ownership of your lists, content and images. You give ABTA a licence to store, copy, display and send them, and to keep sent campaigns in the public archive, for the purpose of providing the service to you. The licence ends when you remove the content, subject to section 12.

You confirm that you have the right to use everything you upload, including images, text and logos, and that it does not infringe anyone’s rights.

ABTA and its licensors own the platform, the templates and the AMI Mailroom name. You may use the templates in your own campaigns but may not copy or resell them.

10. Data protection

For your subscribers’ data you are the controller and ABTA is your processor. The data processing terms apply. You are responsible for your own privacy notice, for the lawful basis you rely on and for answering your subscribers’ requests.

ABTA’s own use of personal data is explained in the privacy policy.

11. The service and our responsibility

ABTA provides the service with reasonable care and skill. It does not promise uninterrupted service or any rate of delivery, opening or response, because mailbox providers decide where mail lands. We will tell you of planned maintenance that affects sending where we can.

ABTA is not responsible for your content, for your list or for how you treat your subscribers.

Nothing in these terms limits liability for death or personal injury caused by negligence, for fraud or fraudulent misrepresentation, or for anything else that cannot lawfully be limited.

Subject to that, ABTA is not liable for loss of profit, revenue, goodwill or data, or for any indirect or consequential loss. ABTA’s total liability to you under these terms in any 12-month period is limited to the fees you paid to ABTA for AMI Mailroom in that period.

You will compensate ABTA for losses, claims and penalties arising from your list, your content or your conduct towards a subscriber, including a breach of the laws that apply to your email.

12. Ending these terms

You may stop sending and close your account at any time from your account page. You can export your data first. Closing the account deletes your lists, subscribers, send and delivery records, drafts, images and saved blocks. Campaigns you have already sent remain in the archive unless you ask for them to be removed.

ABTA may end these terms by giving you 30 days’ notice by email, or straight away if you breach them in a way that cannot be put right, if you are unlawfully sending email or if ABTA is required to by law.

Sections that by their nature continue after the terms end, including sections 9, 11 and 14, continue to apply.

13. Changes to these terms

ABTA may update these terms. For a change that reduces your rights or adds to your obligations we will give you at least 30 days’ notice by email to the address on your account. For other changes the new version applies when published, and the version number and date at the top show which one you are reading.

If you do not accept a change, you can close your account before it takes effect.

14. General

Notices from ABTA to you are sent to the email address on your account. Notices to ABTA go to ami@abta.africa.

These terms, with the documents they refer to, are the whole agreement between us about AMI Mailroom. If a part is found to be unenforceable, the rest continues. A delay in enforcing a right does not waive it. You may not transfer your account or these terms without our written consent. A person who is not party to these terms has no right to enforce them.

These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales. We will try to settle any dispute by negotiation and then by mediation. If that fails, the courts of England and Wales have jurisdiction, although either of us may seek urgent relief from any competent court.
