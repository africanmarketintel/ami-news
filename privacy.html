Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026  ·  Applies to African Market Intelligence and all its services

In plain terms. We are African Business Trade Association Limited. We publish African Market Intelligence and run the services under it. We collect what we need to publish, to send what you asked for and to operate those services. We do not sell personal information. One account signs you in to all of our services, so some of what we hold about you is shared between them.

1. Who we are

African Market Intelligence (AMI) is a service of African Business Trade Association Limited ("ABTA", "we", "us"), a company registered in England and Wales, United Kingdom, company number 16401954. Registered office: Fifth Floor, 167–169 Great Portland Street, London, England, W1W 5PF.

ABTA is the data controller for personal information collected through ami.abta.africa and every service on it.

2. What this policy covers

This is the policy for AMI as a whole. It covers the website, the weekly newsletter and your AMI account.

Each service also has its own notice, because each one does something different with personal information. Where a service notice and this policy differ, the service notice is the more specific of the two and applies to that service.

3. One account across our services

This is the part a single service notice cannot explain, so it is set out here.

Our services share one sign-in system. When you create an account on any of them, that account identifies you on all of them. In practice this means:

  • Your email address and sign-in records are held once and are visible to each service you use.
  • Signing in to one service signs you in to the others.
  • What a service holds beyond your sign-in is kept separately. A Dealroom membership does not make your Mailroom campaigns visible to the Dealroom, and the reverse is also true.
  • Closing an account on one service does not close the others. If you want everything removed, say so and we will do that.

4. The newsletter

If you subscribe to the AMI weekly newsletter we hold your email address, any name you give us, the record of how and when you subscribed, and whether you open or click the emails we send.

Where you are an individual subscriber we rely on your consent. Where you are a corporate subscriber we rely on our legitimate interest in sending business-to-business market intelligence you asked for or would reasonably expect.

Every newsletter carries an unsubscribe link, and it works immediately. You can also reply to any newsletter and ask to be removed. Once you unsubscribe we keep your address on a suppression list so that we do not email you again by accident. That record is kept precisely so we can honour your decision.

Where the newsletter is sent from

The newsletter is currently sent through third-party email platforms, and your address is held by them in order to deliver it. We are moving this to our own infrastructure. The platforms in use are named in section 7.

5. The website

You can read AMI without an account. Some reporting is for paid members, and reading that requires signing in.

We use your browser's storage to keep you signed in, to remember your preferences and to remember how many free articles you have read. This is not advertising tracking, and we do not use it to build a profile of you for anyone else.

7. Who we share information with

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

We use service providers who process personal information on our instructions:

  • A cloud database and sign-in provider, which holds your account, your sign-in records and any files you upload to our services.
  • An email delivery provider, which sends campaigns on behalf of Mailroom members and records whether each message was delivered, bounced or was reported as spam.
  • A second email delivery provider, which sends transactional messages such as sign-in codes, confirmations and notifications.
  • A payment provider, which takes and holds card details and processes payments. We never see or hold your card number.
  • A website hosting provider, which serves these pages and receives the technical information any web server receives, such as your IP address.
  • Two newsletter platforms, which hold subscriber email addresses and engagement records in order to send the weekly newsletter. We are moving this to our own infrastructure.

Each of these acts only on our instructions and may not use your information for anything of its own. We will tell you the name of any of them if you ask.

We also share information where the law requires it, or to establish or defend legal claims.

8. International transfers

Some of our service providers store or process information outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, we rely on the safeguards required by UK data protection law.

9. How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as we need it for the purpose we collected it, and then delete it, except where the law requires us to keep it longer.

Two exceptions worth stating plainly. Suppression records are kept indefinitely, because their whole purpose is to stop us contacting you again. Records needed for accounting and tax are kept for the period the law requires.

10. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, to receive it in a portable form, and to withdraw consent where we relied on it. You also have an absolute right to object to direct marketing, and if you exercise it we must stop.

To exercise any of these, email ami@abta.africa. We will respond within one month.

11. Complaints

If you are unhappy with how we use your information, please contact us first so we can try to put it right. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.
